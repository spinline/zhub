Restart the hub
Saves the state and starts zhub again; the network stays up for the devices and the page reconnects in about 10 s.
Factory reset a Hue light by serial number
The 6-character code printed on the light (0-9, A-F). Separate several with commas. The light must be powered; after the reset it joins on its own. Takes about 20 s.
